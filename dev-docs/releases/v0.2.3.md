# Song Summoner Revival v0.2.3

The biggest update since the first release: **play the whole game with a
controller**, a new **Setup menu** on both Windows and Android, your own
music library with a rebuilt iPod picker, and saves that survive an
uninstall on Android.

> **You still need your own copy of the game.** The IPA is not included in
> the download, the APK or the Windows build. You pick your copy the first
> time you start.

## Downloads

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| Android (arm64) | `song-summoner-0.2.3.apk` |
| Windows (64-bit) | The Windows zip: unzip it anywhere and run `touchHLE.exe` |

Updating on Android: install over the old version. If you played the first
release, pick your old `SongSummoner` folder as the save folder and your
saves come along.

## What's new

### 🎮 Controller support, everywhere

- **Menus:** every button menu, dialog, list, the title drum, the Options
  and Help screens, the card list and the shop. The D-pad moves by where
  buttons are on screen.
- **Dialogs never wrap:** left is always **No**, right is always **Yes**.
  Up and down still wrap in a column.
- **Battle:** a free tile cursor, move and attack select with the game's own
  previews, the command ring, deploying troopers, L1/R1 to step through
  your units, L2/R2 to zoom the map, Start for the battle menu.
- **World map:** move along the roads, cycle locations, and choose from the
  location and town menus.
- **Cutscenes:** Start skips.
- **Shop password:** the game's on-screen keyboard works with the D-pad.
  You can also type it on a real keyboard (Windows, or a USB or Bluetooth
  keyboard on Android) or your phone's own keyboard. Pick which in Setup >
  Game > Shop password.
- **Button icons** match your pad: Xbox, PlayStation or Nintendo.
- Pick which face button confirms: bottom (default) or right.

### 👁️ An easier-to-see cursor

Setup > Game > Cursor lets you choose how the controller's cursor looks:

- **Look:** *Like the game*, *Outlined* or *Bold*.
- **Colour:** *Gold*, *White*, *Yellow* or *Sky blue*.

Every colour sits on a dark edge, so the cursor stands out on any
background without needing to tell colours apart. It's friendlier for
colour-blind players and easier to read on small screens.

### ⚙️ The Setup menu

One menu for both platforms, drawn over the paused game. It opens by itself
on the first launch; after that press **F2**, hold **Select + Start** on a
controller, or tap the **gear** on Android.

- **Game:** change the game file, button icons, FPS counter, fullscreen
  (Windows), the fading gear (Android), cursor look and shop password.
- **Music:** add and remove music folders, rescan, list scroll speed.
- **Controller:** move any action to another button, stick dead zone.
- **Keyboard:** two keys per action, all remappable. Now on Android too.
- **Data & help:** open the data folder, make a bug report, back up and
  restore saves, and a button tester.
- **Credits:** everyone this is built on, and a tip jar (below).

Touch works throughout: one tap chooses, and you can drag to scroll long
tabs like Credits.

### 🎵 Your music, your troopers

- Point the game at folders of your own **mp3, m4a, aac, wav or caf** files.
  Tags and embedded cover art are read; each subfolder becomes a playlist.
- The iPod picker is rebuilt in the style of the original, with the game's
  own art: Song, Artist, Album and Playlist tabs, an A–Z strip, and fighter
  portraits. It works by touch or controller.
- Your trooper's song plays from your file in the palace, in battle and on
  the results screen, and each play counts towards listening points.

### 💾 Android: saves that survive

- The app no longer asks for any storage permission.
- On the first launch you pick your game file, then a **save folder** (for
  example `Documents/SongSummoner`). Saves, settings, backups and bug
  reports are copied there, so they survive an uninstall.

### 🔧 Other fixes

- Windows builds are assembled into a ready-to-run folder, and rebuilding
  never touches your saves or options.
- The game no longer has to be baked into the APK or the Windows build.

## Known issues

- In battle, the attack cursor sometimes jumps up a tile on its own.
- The item list in Edit Troopers can scroll oddly with the D-pad.
- Windows antivirus tools (Avast, AVG, Bitdefender, sometimes Defender) may
  flag `touchHLE.exe`. It's a false positive from the emulator's JIT; see the
  README.

Found something else? Setup > Data & help > **Make a bug report** creates a
file you can attach to an issue. It never contains the game or your saves.
